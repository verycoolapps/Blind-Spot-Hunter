## 🇺🇸 English

<br/>

### ◈ What is BLIND SPOT HUNTER?

**BLIND SPOT HUNTER** is a production-ready Claude Code skill engineered as an advanced evidence-based intelligence engine for identifying, investigating, and exposing the gaps between who you *think* you are, and who you *actually* are.

This is NOT a motivational chatbot. This is NOT a life coach simulator. This is NOT a machine that invents psychological-sounding weaknesses to feel impressive.

This is an **investigative intelligence system** that reveals:

| What You Believe | ←→ | What You Actually Demonstrate |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Your Intentions | ←→ | Your Actual Outcomes |
| Your Self-Image | ←→ | What Others Experience |
| Your Assumptions | ←→ | The Evidence Behind Them |
| Your Confidence | ←→ | The Reality Beneath It |

<br/>

### ◈ Why This Exists

Most tools tell you what you want to hear.

**BLIND SPOT HUNTER** tells you what you need to know.

Built on a foundation of **15 investigative dimensions**, a **7-stage investigation engine**, rigorous **evidence classification**, and a strict **anti-slop quality control protocol** — every finding is specific, evidence-grounded, mechanistically explained, and actionable.

Because vague advice is not advice. And comfort without clarity is a trap.

### ◈ Quality Guarantee

Every finding passes this 8-point quality gate before delivery:

- [ ] Specific to your situation
- [ ] Has identifiable supporting evidence
- [ ] Explains the underlying mechanism (not just symptoms)
- [ ] Distinguishable from every other finding
- [ ] Considers alternative explanations
- [ ] Can be tested or investigated
- [ ] Leads to meaningful improvement
- [ ] Would NOT apply to almost everyone without modification

**If a finding fails any gate — it gets revised or removed.**

<br/>

### ◈ Safety & Ethics

- ✅ Investigates patterns, never diagnoses conditions
- ✅ Presents evidence levels explicitly
- ✅ Considers alternative explanations for every finding
- ✅ Respects the difference between limitations and environmental constraints
- ✅ Refers to qualified professionals when concerns exceed scope
- ✅ Uses zero shame as a motivational mechanism
